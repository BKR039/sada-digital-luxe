# SADA OS — SADA Intelligence & Analytics KPI Dictionary
## Authoritative Metric Definitions, Formulas, Data Sources & Reconciliation Rules

**Document Version**: 2.0 (Executive Command Center)  
**System**: SADA OS — Financial OS & Executive Intelligence  
**Authoritative Ledger Architecture**: Single Source of Truth (SSOT) via Supabase / PostgreSQL  
**Currency Standard**: Tunisian Dinar (TND) — 3 decimal places (Millimes, e.g. `1,250.500 TND`)

---

## 1. Architectural Principles & Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)**:
   All metrics are derived directly from canonical operational and financial tables:
   - `income_transactions` (Authoritative Cash Inflows)
   - `expense_transactions` (Authoritative Cash & Accrued Outflows)
   - `salary_payments` (Payroll Obligations & Disbursements)
   - `invoices` (Receivables & Billing Records)
   - `sponsoring_campaigns` (Client Advertising Budgets & Ad Spend)
   - `financial_accounts` (Treasury Balances & Account Reserves)
   - `project_tasks` (Operational Work, Deliverables, Statuses)
   - `work_sessions` (Direct Labor Hours & Time Logs)
   - `package_catalog` (Commercial Retainers & Standard Deliverables)
   - `commissions` (Sales Incentives & Closer Payouts)

2. **Deduplication Invariants**:
   - **Salaries**: Any salary payment linked to an expense transaction (`linked_expense_id IS NOT NULL`) is deduplicated to prevent double-counting against total expenses.
   - **Invoices**: Unpaid invoices represent *Accounts Receivable* and expected cash; settled invoices generate linked records in `income_transactions`. Invoiced amounts and cash collections are never combined into a single number.
   - **Sponsoring & Ad Budgets**: Client ad spend funds deposited for Meta/TikTok/Google campaigns are segregated from SADA's agency creative retainer revenue.

3. **Data Completeness & Zero-Fabrication**:
   - No metric may award a 100% margin simply because direct cost records are absent. When costs are zero or unrecorded for an active package or client, the system flags `isIncompleteCostData: true` and marks the status as **Incomplete Cost Data**.
   - Direct labor costs with small millime values (e.g. 4 minutes = 0.600 TND) must never format with false negative signs or render as `-0.000 TND`.

## 3. Financial Statements & Profitability Matrix

### 3.1 Headline Revenue vs Client Attributed Revenue Reconciliation
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Headline Total Revenue} = \text{Client Attributed Retainers} + \text{Direct Agency Inflows (Unallocated)}$$
- **Authoritative Invariant**:
  When income transactions have `client_id = NULL` (such as direct consulting, training, or ad-hoc agency inflows), they are flagged as `Direct Agency Inflows (Unallocated)` and rendered as an explicit reconciliation entry in the Client Profitability matrix.
  This guarantees that:
  $$\sum (\text{Client Rows}) + \text{Unallocated Row} \equiv \text{Headline Revenue}$$
  to the exact millime.

### 3.2 Client Profitability Metrics
For each client active in the period:
1. **Collected / Retainer Revenue**: Total income received from client plus active monthly contractual retainer.
2. **Direct Production Costs**: Total logged hours from `work_sessions` multiplied by worker hourly cost rates (`hourly_rate_tnd`).
3. **Sponsoring Costs**: Ad spend executed by SADA on behalf of client (`sponsoring_campaigns.spent_amount`).
4. **Sales Commission**: Closer and sales commission payable for securing the client contract (`commissions.amount`).
5. **Contribution Profit**:
   $$\text{Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Direct Costs} - \text{Sponsoring} - \text{Commissions}$$
6. **Contribution Margin %**:
   $$\text{Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Contribution Profit}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100$$
7. **Cost Completeness Badge**:
   - `Verified`: When direct hours, sponsoring, and commissions are actively tracked.
   - `Incomplete`: When direct costs are 0 and no work sessions have been recorded. Margin % is suppressed or flagged.
8. **Delivery Health Badge**:
   - `ON_TRACK`: 0 overdue tasks, completion rate $\ge 70\%$.
   - `AT_RISK`: 1 to 2 overdue tasks or blocked items.
   - `DELAYED`: $\ge 3$ overdue tasks or milestone missed.
9. **Payment Risk Badge**:
   - `LOW`: 0 overdue invoices, all payments on schedule.
   - `MODERATE`: Invoices pending within 15 days of due date.
   - `HIGH`: Overdue invoices exceeding 15 days past due.

### 3.3 Package & Service Profitability
- **Selling Price**: Catalog baseline retainer price (`package_catalog.price_tnd`).
- **Standard Direct Cost**:
  $$\text{Unit Cost} = \text{Employee Cost} + \text{Freelancer Cost} + \text{Tech Cost} + \text{Sponsoring Budget} + \text{Commission}$$
- **Margin Guard**:
  If all cost components equal 0, `isIncompleteCostData = true`. Gross margin percent returns `null` or an amber warning rather than an erroneous 100%.

## 4. Cash Flow & Treasury Architecture

1. **Inflows**: All cleared bank, postal, and cash deposits recorded in `income_transactions`.
2. **Outflows**: All cleared vendor payments, payroll disbursements, subscription charges, and tax payments in `expense_transactions`.
3. **Net Cash Movement**: $\text{Inflows} - \text{Outflows}$.
4. **Treasury Reserves**:
   - **Available Cash**: Unrestricted operating funds available in commercial bank accounts.
   - **Reserved Cash**: Mandatory reserves allocated for:
     - Client Sponsoring Escrow (funds received for ad platforms not yet billed by Meta/Google)
     - VAT & Tax Obligations (estimated withholding)
     - Next Payroll Run (committed 30-day salary pool)
   - **Upcoming Obligations**: Unpaid vendor invoices, upcoming software renewals, and pending commission disbursements within the next 30 days.

## 5. Operations Intelligence Pipeline & Health

1. **Pipeline Stages**:
   - `Planned`: Tasks created in backlog, sprint, or package scope.
   - `In Production`: Tasks actively assigned and worked on (`IN_PROGRESS`).
   - `In Review`: Deliverables submitted for internal creative or account manager review (`REVIEW`).
   - `Approved`: Work validated internally, awaiting publishing or delivery (`APPROVED`).
   - `Published / Delivered`: Work released to client or published across social channels (`COMPLETED`).
2. **Operational Key Counters**:
   - **Tasks Due Today**: Real-time count of tasks with `due_date = CURRENT_DATE`.
   - **Overdue Tasks**: Tasks with `due_date < CURRENT_DATE` and `status != 'COMPLETED'`.
   - **Upcoming Deadlines (Next 7 Days)**: Tasks scheduled for delivery between `TODAY + 1` and `TODAY + 7`.
   - **Blocked Tasks**: Tasks flagged with dependencies or client input required.
   - **Awaiting Review**: Deliverables in creative queue waiting for Art Director / Account Director sign-off.
   - **Ready to Publish**: Client-approved reels, designs, and copies awaiting scheduled release.

## 7. Access Control & Authorization (RBAC Shield)

- **Super Admin & Admin**: Full read/write access to Management Analytics, Executive Command Strip, P&L, Cash Flow, Salary Breakdowns, and Audit Logs.
- **Worker / Employee**:
  - Strictly blocked from `/admin/analytics`.
  - Route automatically redirects workers to `/admin/my-work`.
  - Server endpoints and client services redact all company P&L, employee compensation, client retainer revenues, and account balances from worker contexts.
